Fall Tournament · Group A vs Group B Doubles League

Format

  • This is Group A playing against Group B — every pair in Group A plays every pair in Group B once: 16 league matches across 4 rounds.
  • League results seed the playoffs, split into Division 1 (top 2 per group) and Division 2 (bottom 2 per group).

Match Format

  • Best of 2 sets — each set first to 6 games, tiebreak at 6–6.
  • If sets split 1–1 → 10-point match tiebreak (win by 2) decides the match.

Scoring

ResultWinnerLoser
Win 2–0 (straight sets)3 pts0
Win 1–1 via match tiebreak2 pts1 pt

Every match distributes 3 points. Max 12 points per pair (4 matches).

Standings (within each group)

  • 1. League points (3/2/1)
  • 2. Game difference (games won − games lost, sets only)

Playoffs

  • Division 1: Ranks 1–2 per group → A1 v B2, A2 v B1 → Championship Final
  • Division 2: Ranks 3–4 per group → A3 v B4, A4 v B3 → Div 2 Final
